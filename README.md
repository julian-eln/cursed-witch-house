# Core Idea: 
Procedurally generated church where you have to escape a witch who has cursed the church.  

# World & Narrative: 
user finds themself taking shelter in a haunted church, cursed by a witch, and needs to find a way out to avoid the witch’s wrath for trespassing into her home. The user needs to find their way out of the witch’s home before they’re cursed to become part of her home.
